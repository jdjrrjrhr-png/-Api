Place your TopdownMap.png in this directory.

The map image should:
- Cover the full playable area of your map
- Be a top-down orthographic view
- PNG format recommended for quality

The map boundaries (X_min, X_max, Z_min, Z_max) in config.json
must correspond exactly to the world boundaries this image covers.

The normalization algorithm:
  left% = ((x - X_min) / (X_max - X_min)) * 100
  top%  = ((z - Z_min) / (Z_max - Z_min)) * 100

places player markers at the correct pixel position over the image.
